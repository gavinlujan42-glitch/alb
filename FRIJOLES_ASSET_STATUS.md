# Frijoles Fire visual asset status

Verified 2026-08-20.

## Current delivery model
The three rights-cleared historical context images are intentionally referenced from Wikimedia Commons rather than copied into this repository. They are therefore not local binary assets in GitHub. This avoids unnecessary republication while retaining source/licensing provenance.

1. Las Conchas Fire across upper Frijoles landscape (2011) — Wikimedia Commons / U.S. federal public-domain context.
2. NASA/ISS New Mexico orbital context (2011) — Wikimedia Commons / U.S. federal public-domain context.
3. Frijoles Canyon post-fire flood documentation (2013) — Wikimedia Commons / National Park Service public-domain context.

The 2026 Frijoles Fire perimeter is not a static image. It is loaded live from the public NIFC/WFIGS ArcGIS FeatureServer and rendered spatially with Leaflet.

## Editorial rule
Do not copy social-media or news photographs merely because they are publicly viewable. Add new imagery only after an explicit public-domain, CC0, compatible Creative Commons license, or direct permission is verified. Historic/context images must remain clearly labeled as not depicting the 2026 Frijoles Fire.

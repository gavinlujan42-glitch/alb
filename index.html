Uncle Zio's
Wandering Signal

TZ TOZO, pronounced and styled like a roadside call sign, is ALB's fictional wandering correspondent: a little Uncle Zio, a little high-desert hobo philosopher, a little reporter who follows the smell of coffee, rain, chile and trouble. Española is home territory, but the road is his newsroom.

He does not chase active wildfire into closure zones. He reports from the safe edge, from towns and roads where the smoke, fear, memory and human story actually live.

The Wandering Signal

TZ TOZO is ALB's fictional wandering New Mexico correspondent. Española is home territory, but geography is negotiable. He may turn up in Burque, Taos, Gallup, a diner in Las Vegas, beside an acequia or wherever the story catches him.

Part road reporter, part high-desert observer, TOZO looks for the human signal beneath official language: water, fire, work, politics, landscape and the consequences people actually live with.

# Frijoles Fire spatial feature — media rights ledger

ALB intentionally does **not** treat publicly viewable social-media or news photographs as public domain.

## Embedded images

1. `ConchasFire flame&smoke across upper Frijoles IMG 0740 cda.jpg`
   - Source: Wikimedia Commons, Category: Las Conchas Fire
   - Used only as **historical 2011 landscape/fire context**, not as a photograph of the 2026 Frijoles Fire.
   - Commons archive identifies federal/public-domain material; source page is linked in the caption.

2. `ISS028-E-10043 - View of New Mexico.jpg`
   - Source: Wikimedia Commons Las Conchas Fire archive / NASA ISS imagery.

3. `Frijoles Canyon flood 1.jpg`
   - Source: National Park Service via Wikimedia Commons.

## GIS

- Current fire perimeter is requested client-side from the public NIFC/WFIGS `WFIGS_Interagency_Perimeters_Current` ArcGIS FeatureServer.
- Basemap is OpenStreetMap and attribution is displayed on the map.
- The page is explicitly marked non-operational and directs users to official incident/evacuation sources.

## Policy for future additions

Before embedding any current Frijoles Fire photograph, verify an explicit reuse basis such as U.S. federal public domain, CC0, CC BY, or another license compatible with the site. A photograph being visible on Facebook, Instagram, X, Reddit, a news site, or a public webpage is **not** sufficient permission to republish it.

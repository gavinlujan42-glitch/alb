04 // Terrain & human geography

WHY THIS PLACE MATTERS

The fire is burning in steep ponderosa-timber terrain, with dead and down fuels, inside the Pecos Wilderness on the Española Ranger District. The western side faces a necklace of communities and cultural landscapes: Cundiyó, Cañon Chimayó, Cerro Piñon, Nambé, Cordova and the Española Valley. Smoke behaves spatially too, draining and settling into valleys overnight. That is why a fire miles away can transform the morning atmosphere in Santa Fe and Española.
